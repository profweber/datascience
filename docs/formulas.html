Appendix B — Formula Collection

This appendix collects the formulas of the script, ordered by task. It contains no explanations: what a formula means and when it applies is explained in the chapter named in the last column.

Notation: \(x_1, \dots, x_n\) are observations, \(n\) is the sample size, \(\bar x\) the mean, \(\tilde x\) the median and \(s\) the sample standard deviation. \(\sum_i\) runs over all observations.

B.1 Describing data

Quantity Formula Used in
Mean \(\bar x = \dfrac{1}{n}\sum_i x_i\) Ch. 2
Sample standard deviation \(s = \sqrt{\dfrac{1}{n-1}\sum_i (x_i - \bar x)^2}\) Ch. 2
Interquartile range \(\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1\) (quartiles: 25 % and 75 % quantiles) Ch. 2
Median absolute deviation \(\text{MAD} = \operatorname{median}\big(\lvert x_1 - \tilde x \rvert, \dots, \lvert x_n - \tilde x \rvert\big)\) Ch. 2
IQR rule (Tukey) flag \(x_i\) if \(x_i < Q_1 - 1.5\,\text{IQR}\) or \(x_i > Q_3 + 1.5\,\text{IQR}\) Ch. 2
Robust z-score \(z_i = \dfrac{x_i - \tilde x}{1.4826 \cdot \text{MAD}}\), flag \(x_i\) if \(\lvert z_i \rvert > 3.5\) Ch. 2
Pearson correlation \(r_{xy} = \dfrac{\sum_i (x_i - \bar x)(y_i - \bar y)}{\sqrt{\sum_i (x_i - \bar x)^2}\,\sqrt{\sum_i (y_i - \bar y)^2}}\) Ch. 1

B.2 Probability and lifetimes

Quantity Formula Used in
Distribution function, density \(F(x) = P(X \le x)\), \(\quad f(x) = F'(x)\) Ch. 3
Probability of an interval \(P(a < X \le b) = F(b) - F(a) = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx\) Ch. 3
Quantile \(F^{-1}(p)\): the value below which a share \(p\) lies Ch. 3
Expected value, variance \(E[X] = \int x\,f(x)\,dx\), \(\quad \text{Var}(X) = E\big[(X - E[X])^2\big]\) Ch. 3
Central limit theorem \(\bar X_n\) is approximately normal with mean \(\mu\) and standard deviation \(\sigma/\sqrt{n}\) Ch. 3
Reliability (lifetime \(X\)) \(R(t) = 1 - F(t) = P(X > t)\) Ch. 3
Weibull distribution \(F(t) = 1 - \exp\left[-\left(\dfrac{t}{\eta}\right)^{\beta}\right]\) Ch. 3
Three-parameter Weibull \(F(t) = 1 - \exp\left[-\left(\dfrac{t - t_0}{\eta}\right)^{\beta}\right]\) for \(t > t_0\), \(\quad F(t) = 0\) for \(t \le t_0\) Ch. 3
Hazard rate \(h(t) = \dfrac{f(t)}{R(t)}\); Weibull: \(h(t) = \dfrac{\beta}{\eta}\left(\dfrac{t}{\eta}\right)^{\beta - 1}\) Ch. 3
B10 life (Weibull) \(B_{10} = \eta\,(-\ln 0.9)^{1/\beta}\) Ch. 3
Log-likelihood \(\ell(\theta) = \sum_i \ln f(x_i; \theta)\), maximised by the estimate \(\hat\theta\) Ch. 3
Akaike information criterion \(\mathrm{AIC} = 2k - 2\,\ell(\hat\theta)\), \(\quad k\) = number of fitted parameters Ch. 3
Bayes’ theorem \(P(F \mid A) = \dfrac{P(A \mid F)\,P(F)}{P(A)}\), \(\quad P(A) = P(A \mid F)\,P(F) + P(A \mid \bar F)\,P(\bar F)\) Ch. 3

B.3 Confidence intervals

Quantity Formula Used in
Standard error of the mean \(\text{SE} = \dfrac{s}{\sqrt n}\) Ch. 3
95 % confidence interval for the mean \(\bar x \pm t_{n-1,\,0.975}\,\dfrac{s}{\sqrt n}\) Ch. 3
Bootstrap (percentile method) 95 % interval: 2.5 % and 97.5 % quantiles of the bootstrap replicates Ch. 3
95 % confidence interval for a regression coefficient \(\hat\beta_j \pm t_{n-p-1,\,0.975}\,\text{SE}(\hat\beta_j)\) Ch. 5

B.4 Hypothesis tests

Quantity Formula Used in
Welch t-test \(t = \dfrac{\bar x_1 - \bar x_2}{\sqrt{s_1^2/n_1 + s_2^2/n_2}}\) Ch. 4
Degrees of freedom (Welch) \(\nu = \dfrac{\left(s_1^2/n_1 + s_2^2/n_2\right)^2}{\dfrac{(s_1^2/n_1)^2}{n_1 - 1} + \dfrac{(s_2^2/n_2)^2}{n_2 - 1}}\) Ch. 4
Range of \(\nu\) \(\min(n_1, n_2) - 1 \le \nu \le n_1 + n_2 - 2\) Ch. 4
Cohen’s d \(d = \dfrac{\bar x_1 - \bar x_2}{s_p}\), \(\quad s_p = \sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2 + s_2^2}{2}}\) Ch. 4
One-way ANOVA (\(k\) groups, \(N\) observations) \(F = \dfrac{\sum_j n_j(\bar x_j - \bar x)^2/(k-1)}{\sum_j \sum_i (x_{ij} - \bar x_j)^2/(N-k)}\) Ch. 4
Chi-squared test \(\chi^2 = \sum \dfrac{(O - E)^2}{E}\), \(\quad E = \dfrac{\text{row total} \cdot \text{column total}}{\text{total count}}\) Ch. 4
False alarms in \(m\) independent tests \(P(\text{at least one}) = 1 - (1 - \alpha)^m\) Ch. 4
Bonferroni correction significance level \(\alpha/m\) for each of \(m\) tests Ch. 4
Sample size per group (two-sample t-test) \(n \approx \dfrac{2\,(z_{1-\alpha/2} + z_{1-\beta})^2}{d^2}\), \(\quad d = \delta/\sigma\); \(\quad n \approx 16/d^2\) for \(\alpha = 0.05\) and power 0.8 Ch. 4

B.5 Regression

Quantity Formula Used in
Multiple linear regression \(y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_{i1} + \dots + \beta_p x_{ip} + \varepsilon_i\), \(\quad \varepsilon_i \sim N(0, \sigma^2)\) independent Ch. 5
Least squares estimate \(\hat\beta = (X^\top X)^{-1} X^\top y\), \(\quad \widehat{\text{Cov}}(\hat\beta) = \hat\sigma^2 (X^\top X)^{-1}\) Ch. 5
Residual variance \(\hat\sigma^2 = \dfrac{1}{n - p - 1}\sum_i (y_i - \hat y_i)^2\) Ch. 5
Coefficient of determination \(R^2 = 1 - \dfrac{\sum_i (y_i - \hat y_i)^2}{\sum_i (y_i - \bar y)^2}\) Ch. 5
Variance inflation factor \(\text{VIF}_j = \dfrac{1}{1 - R_j^2}\), \(\quad R_j^2\): \(R^2\) of \(x_j\) regressed on all other inputs Ch. 5
Nonlinear least squares \(\hat\theta = \arg\min_{\theta} \sum_i \big(y_i - f(x_i; \theta)\big)^2\) Ch. 5
