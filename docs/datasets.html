# Datasets {#sec-datasets}

All datasets used in this script are publicly available. Please cite the original sources when you use them.

| Dataset | Domain | Size | License | Chapters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Combined Cycle Power Plant](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/294/combined+cycle+power+plant) | power generation | 9,568 rows, 4 features | CC BY 4.0 | 1, 6 |

| [Gas Turbine CO and NOx Emission](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/551/gas+turbine+co+and+nox+emission+data+set) | gas turbine emissions | 36,733 rows, 2011–2015 | CC BY 4.0 | 4 |
| [AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/601/ai4i+2020+predictive+maintenance+dataset) | manufacturing (synthetic) | 10,000 rows, 5 failure modes | CC BY 4.0 | 4, 7 |
| [Concrete Compressive Strength](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/165/concrete+compressive+strength) | materials, civil engineering | 1,030 rows, 8 features | CC BY 4.0 | 5 |

| [Condition Monitoring of Hydraulic Systems](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/447/condition+monitoring+of+hydraulic+systems) | hydraulic test rig | 2,205 cycles, 17 sensors up to 100 Hz | CC BY 4.0 | 9, 10 |
| [CWRU Bearing Data Center](https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter) | rolling bearings, vibration | normal and faulty bearings, 0–3 hp | freely available | 10 |
| [Open Power System Data – Time Series](https://data.open-power-system-data.org/time_series/) | European power grid | 32 countries, 2015–2020, hourly | open (sources: ENTSO-E and others) | 11 |
